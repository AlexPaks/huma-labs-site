Date: 2026-08-17

Purpose:

- Record the approved Hebrew and English terminology used across the HUMA website during Phase 3.
- Keep the homepage, Organizational Insight, forms, validation, and accessibility language consistent.

## Consistency notes

- Keep `Presence`, `Resilience`, `Adaptability`, and `Leadership` capitalized when used as formal capability labels in English.
- Keep `Discover`, `Design`, and `Act` uppercase when they appear as process labels in UI chrome.
- Use `organizational challenge` and `human capability` together when describing HUMA's thesis.
- Do not replace HUMA's language with generic AI or generic SaaS vocabulary.
- Do not introduce unapproved SEO targeting language during Phase 3.
